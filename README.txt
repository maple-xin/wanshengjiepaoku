PUMPKIN POST — Moonlight Delivery

A standalone Halloween 3D boating game adapted from the user's Tideline Rush V7. Open index.html in a WebGL-capable browser. All fonts, model data, programs and audio are embedded; no CDN or external server is required. Portrait phone play is supported. Upload the extracted folder to static HTTPS hosting to share it.

PLAY
Deliver the Treats is the story route. Read the pumpkin parcel note, steer through at least three lantern gates, and deliver the treats to the friendly ghost keeper's mailbox. Festival Race offers Pumpkin Quay, Ghostlight Marsh and Moonlit Manor as separate courses. Drag left/right to steer, hold BREW and tap JUMP. Tap again while airborne for a trick. Low pumpkin crates can be jumped or avoided; crossing brooms should be given room. Follow the dotted star landing charm and lantern markers.

Mint wisps and candy restore brew energy; rose imps slow the boat. Bubble pools provide a moon lift. Meet the three friendly Boo Buddies near the candy trails to earn a one-hit Boo Shield. The shield safely absorbs one collision; it never stacks beyond one charge. The shield ring and toast show when it is ready or consumed. The stone moon gate offers a shortcut; the optional Ghost Cove contains a clue and hidden candy cache. Successful delivery earns keepsakes; extra discovery and a clean run earn the other two. Collection, loadout and sound settings use a separate Pumpkin Post browser-storage namespace.

Costumed couriers and boats retain different actual performance/abilities. The Vampire Acrobat wears a wine-lined cape, high collar, waistcoat and fangs. The Pumpkin Tinkerer wears a ribbed pumpkin work helmet and apron. Batwing Dash has folding bat fins that tuck near narrow gates. Ghostlight Drifter has a sheet-ghost fan shroud, rotating rotor and pumpkin lamps. Selection icons match these model designs. The original Witch Courier is steady and has a generous candy reach; the Vampire Acrobat steers faster in air and earns more trick points; the Pumpkin Tinkerer can repair the boat. Lantern Skiff is forgiving, Batwing Dash is fast, and Ghostlight Drifter is agile.

Desktop: A/D or arrow keys steer; Shift boosts; Space jumps; Escape pauses or returns. Retry restarts promptly. Pause cancels held boost and steering. Music and effects have separate switches. A first click enables browser audio. Cover and selection screens use the Pumpkin Post Waltz; each scene has its own original seasonal score. Page blur stops audio. The theme is cute and lightly spooky, with no jump scares, blood or flashing effects.
